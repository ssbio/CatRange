CatRange

Predicting enzyme kcat / KM ranges from sequence + substrate

A tool from the SSB Lab · Published in PNAS Nexus

Recommended: run it yourself in Google Colab

Free, no queue, and runs on Google's infrastructure. Most users should start here.

Alternative: submit a job to the lab's queue

No Google account needed. The lab's own server runs the same CLEAN + CatRange pipeline as the Colab notebook and hands you back a shareable results link. This depends on the lab's compute being available — if it's at capacity we'll tell you immediately and point you back to Colab instead of leaving you waiting.

Up to 10 pairs. Each line: sequence,Isomeric SMILES

Needs sequence and Isomeric SMILES columns. See the example CSV.

Evolution of this work

CatRange's range-prediction approach builds on an earlier binary-classification model, RealKcat, maintained at TKAI-LAB-Mali/CatRange.

Robust Prediction of Enzyme Variant Kinetics with RealKcat. bioRxiv [Preprint]. 2025 Feb 15. doi: 10.1101/2025.02.10.637555. PMID: 39990461 · PMCID: PMC11844551.
