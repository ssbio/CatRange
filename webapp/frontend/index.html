CatRange: Catalytically Aware and Mutation-Resolved Kinetics Prediction To Enable Variational Modeling of Enzyme-Substrate Systems

Give it a protein sequence and a substrate (as SMILES) and get back likely ranges for kcat (how fast) and KM (how much substrate is needed) — for wild-type and mutated sequences alike.

A collaboration between Iowa State University, the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, and the University of South Florida — see how CatRange evolved or meet the team behind it.

Two ways to get your predictions

Pick whichever fits — both run the exact same pipeline underneath.

Recommended: Google Colab

Free, no queue, and runs on Google's computers under your own Google account. This is the easiest way to start, and works for anyone.

Alternative: the lab's queue

No Google account needed. The lab's own server runs the same pipeline and gives you a link to check on your job and download results. If the queue is full, we'll tell you right away and point you back to Colab instead of making you wait.
